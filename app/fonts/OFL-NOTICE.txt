Fonts in this folder
=====================

Anton-Regular.ttf — "Anton", designed by Vernon Adams.
Oswald.ttf — "Oswald", a variable font designed by Vernon Adams,
Kalapi Gajjar-Bordawekar and Cyreal.

Both fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1
(http://scripts.sil.org/OFL), which allows them to be used, modified and
redistributed freely, including in this app. The full license text ships with
the fonts' source repository.

Source: the Google Fonts project (github.com/google/fonts), ofl/anton and
ofl/oswald. The files here are subsets of the upstream fonts, keeping the
Latin glyphs (Basic Latin + Latin-1 Supplement) the app's captions need;
subsetting does not change the license. No Rockstar Games font is used.
